Small lessons, useful knowledge

Explore the basics at your own pace.

01

Understanding your cycle

The menstrual cycle includes several phases. Timing can vary from person to person and month to month.

02

What is ovulation?

Ovulation is when an ovary releases an egg. Tracking can help you learn patterns, but it cannot confirm ovulation on its own.

03

Symptoms & self-care

Rest, hydration, movement, and recording symptoms may help you communicate changes with a healthcare professional.
